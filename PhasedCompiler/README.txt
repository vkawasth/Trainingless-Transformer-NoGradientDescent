Your original builds E_0 with k=D+1 eigenvectors and expects VOCAB > D+1. Your real corpus has VOCAB=1017 and D=256, so k=257 is fine. But I added a guard k = min(D+1, VOCAB-1) — for your vocab that's still 257, so no truncation. Just flagging it: if you ever run a corpus with VOCAB < 257, the spectral embedding would be under-ranked and the phases would misbehave. Not your case today, but worth knowing.
