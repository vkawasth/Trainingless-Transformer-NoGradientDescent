Three-token context sheaf

A model is asked about three yes/no claims X₀, X₁, X₂, two at a time, in separate prompts. Each pair agrees with probability ε. Everything below is one object: a presheaf of probability spaces on the lattice of contexts. Move ε and the position inside the fibre, and watch each layer change together.

1 · Information and contextual data

The context lattice

Each window holds a 2×2 table (shade = probability). Downward arrows are restrictions. The highlighted square X₀X₁ ← X₁ → X₁X₂ is the one Beck–Chevalley is tested on.

2 · Algebraic geometry

Marginal polytope

Coordinates dᵢⱼ = P(Xᵢ ≠ Xⱼ). The marginal map sends the simplex of global laws onto this tetrahedron, the convex hull of the four deterministic patterns. The data point moves along the diagonal. Below ε = 1/3 it leaves through the shaded face.

3 · Probability

The fibre over the data

The eight atoms of the global law. Moving inside the fibre adds s·χ, with χ = (−1)^(x₀+x₁+x₂) (darker bars rise, lighter fall). Outside the polytope no s works: the closest candidate puts negative mass on 000 and 111.

4 · Flow

Conditional expectations

The defect ‖E_A E_B − E_{A∧B}‖ = c is the largest conditional correlation of X₀ and X₂ given X₁. The curve shows it across the fibre.

5 · Support faces

Which face the law sits on

The zero pattern of the law fixes the face of the simplex it lies on: the support-level (tropical) shadow. At the ends of the fibre an atom vanishes and the law drops to a lower face.

6 · Outcome

Glue or obstruction

Exact formulas: with a = (3ε−1)/4 and b = (1−ε)/4, the fibre is p = J + s·χ with J(000) = J(111) = a and every other atom b, for |s| ≤ min(a, b). It is non-empty iff ε ≥ 1/3, the certificate is Φ = Σ P(Xᵢ ≠ Xⱼ) ≤ 2, and at s = 0 the defect is c = |2ε−1|/(2ε). These match checks L1–L8 in check_hallucination.py and Section 16 of Shadows, Fibres and Secants of the Posterior Tower.
